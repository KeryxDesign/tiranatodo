About this event

Every second Saturday Tulla opens its stage to anyone with a song, a poem or a five-minute set. Sign up at the door from 19:30; slots go in order of arrival and each performer gets eight minutes. The audience is a mix of locals and internationals, and the host switches between English and Albanian.

Doors close when the room is full, about sixty people, so come early if you want a seat. The bar stays open until midnight.
