Submit an event

There is no form here. You copy the email below, fill in your event and send it. The fields tell you what to write, so you do not have to guess.

  1. 1

    Copy the email template

    Press the Copy button. The whole message goes to your clipboard, ready to paste.

  2. 2

    Fill in your event

    Write your answer after each field. Field names stay in English. Your text can be in English, Albanian or Italian.

  3. 3

    Send it to us

    Send it from the address where you want our reply. Attach a photo if you have one, up to 5 MB.
