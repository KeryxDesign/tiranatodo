Submit an event

There is no form here. You copy the email below, fill in your event and send it. The fields tell you what to write, so you do not have to guess.

  1. 1

    Copy the email template

    Press the Copy button. The whole message goes to your clipboard, ready to paste.

  2. 2

    Fill in your event

    Write your answer after each field. Field names stay in English. Your text can be in English, Albanian or Italian.

  3. 3

    Send it to us

    Send it from the address where you want our reply. Attach a photo if you have one, up to 5 MB.

How to fill the fields

12 of the 19 fields are required. The rules below cover the formats.

Optional fields These 7 can stay empty. The other 12 all need an answer.

DATE_ENDTIME_ENDTICKET_URLDESCRIPTIONPHOTOORGANIZER_PHONELANGUAGE

Fields not listed here are free text. Write them the way you want.

  • CATEGORYmusicnightlifetheatrecinemaartfoodsportfamilymeetupsoutdoorsother

    Pick one. Write it in lower case, exactly as shown.

  • DATE_START · DATE_ENDYYYY-MM-DD

    Year, then month, then day. Two digits for month and day.

  • TIME_START · TIME_ENDHH:MM

    Use the 24 hour clock. You can skip the start time only if ALL_DAY is YES.

  • ALL_DAY · IS_FREE · CONSENTYESNO

    Only YES or NO. CONSENT must be YES, or we cannot use your event.

  • PRICE500 ALL

    Needed only when IS_FREE is NO. Leave it empty for free events. Always in Lek.

  • PHOTOATTACHEDLINK:<url>NONE

    Pick one of the three. An attached file can be JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB. NONE is fine.

  • DESCRIPTION

    Up to 1500 characters. Shorter is fine.

  • LANGUAGEENSQIT

    The language of your text. If you leave it empty, we use EN.
